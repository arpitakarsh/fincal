# Future Ideas

This document acts as a backlog for visionary features and ideas that are not yet scheduled on the main ROADMAP.

## 1. AMFI Data Synchronization
- **Concept:** Automatically fetch daily NAVs (Net Asset Values) and historical returns from the Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI).
- **Benefit:** Allows the calculator to use real-time data instead of static assumed returns.

## 2. Portfolio Rebalancing Alerts
- **Concept:** If a user's saved portfolio drifts from their target asset allocation (e.g., Equity grows to 80% instead of the target 60%), the app sends an alert to rebalance.
- **Benefit:** Adds active advisory value post-investment.

## 3. Monte Carlo Simulations
- **Concept:** Instead of saying "You need ₹5,000/month assuming a flat 12% return", run 10,000 simulations using historical market volatility.
- **Output:** "You have an 85% probability of reaching your goal with ₹5,000/month."

## 4. Tax Optimization (Tax Harvesting)
- **Concept:** Advise users on Capital Gains Tax implications. Suggest when to book profits (up to the tax-free limit) to optimize long-term returns.

## 5. Goal Priority & Waterfall Funding
- **Concept:** If a user cannot afford the SIP for all their goals, ask them to rank goals by priority. The engine will allocate their available monthly budget to the highest priority goals first.

## 6. AI Portfolio Review
- **Concept:** Users upload their existing mutual fund CAS (Consolidated Account Statement) PDF. The AI parses it, identifies underperforming funds, high expense ratios, or portfolio overlap, and suggests alternatives.

## 7. Notifications (WhatsApp/Email)
- **Concept:** Nudge users to increase their SIPs annually (Step-up reminder), alert them to market corrections as buying opportunities, or remind them of upcoming goal horizons.
